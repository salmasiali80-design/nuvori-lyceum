WHO THIS IS FOR

Not a class you attend. A conversation you prepare for.

You prepare one real case, take a position, and defend it out loud against challenge.

You leave with English you have used, not English you have revised.

Not a beginner course Not level-completion Not exam preparation

HOW IT WORKS

Every session works the same way.

01

Before the lesson

40 minutes

You get the case file. Watch one short video, read one real situation, and decide what you would do.

02

In the lesson

60 minutes

You explain your decision and defend it while I challenge it. The words you need are already in the file.

03

After the lesson

5 minutes

You record your answer to one question and send it to me.
